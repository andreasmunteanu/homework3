Our Story

Simigeria LUCA was born out of a desire to offer passersby a quick, affordable, and delicious meal without ever compromising on quality. Operating under a company with 100% Romanian capital, LUCA has grown from a humble bakery into a massive national network of over 150 locations.

Our Values

  • Freshness: Everything is baked on-site, multiple times a day. We never sell yesterday's products.
  • Affordable quality: Great baked goods should not be a luxury. Our prices stay accessible to everyone.
  • Romanian tradition: We honour classic Romanian recipes — covrigi, plăcinte, cozonac — made the way they have always been made.
  • Community: We support local suppliers and donate unsold products to Banca de Alimente Timișoara every evening.
