Welcome to Luca

Simigeria LUCA is an iconic, widely loved Romanian bakery chain that has taken the country by storm. Famous for its mouthwatering pretzels (covrigi), savory snacks, and pastry products, LUCA offers a warm, nostalgic atmosphere reminiscent of traditional Romanian baking. It is the perfect spot to grab a quick, delicious, and consistent bite, whether you are starting your morning commute or taking an afternoon stroll through the city.

Featured Products

Here are some of our most popular items this month:

  • Covrig cu Cașcaval (Cheese Pretzel) — A fluffy pretzel with a beautifully golden, slightly crunchy, and aromatic baked cheese crust. It is a massive fan-favorite.
  • LUCA Tradițional — A signature staple. This is a classic pretzel dough baked with a warm core of mozzarella that is carefully wrapped in fine ham, all topped with roasted poppy seeds.
  • Merdenea — A beloved traditional Romanian pastry made of flaky, buttery dough filled with savory cheese—perfectly crispy on the outside and soft and warm on the inside.
  • Pizza LUCA — Generously sized slices of pizza baked fresh in-store, combining a rich tomato sauce, melted cheese, and hearty toppings on a soft, pretzel-like dough base.
